# Real pictures and where to find them

Real photographs, paintings, maps and documents beat mock-ups. These archives are free to search.

| Archive | Good for | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Wikimedia Commons | Everything | Built in: Projects → a project → Find real pictures (records licence and author) |
| Library of Congress, loc.gov/free-to-use | Old photos, posters, maps | Browse the sets; each item states its rights |
| The Met, metmuseum.org/art/collection | Paintings, objects | Tick "Open Access" (CC0) |
| Smithsonian, si.edu/openaccess | Objects, science, history | Open Access items are CC0 |
| Rijksmuseum, rijksmuseum.nl/en/rijksstudio | Dutch masters, very large files | Public-domain works download in full size |
| National Gallery of Art, nga.gov/open-access-images | Paintings | Open-access images are public domain |
| NYPL, digitalcollections.nypl.org | Maps, ephemera | Switch on "public domain only" |
| NASA, images.nasa.gov | Space and Earth | Generally free to use |
| Europeana, europeana.eu | European museums in one search | Filter to free re-use |
| Internet Archive, archive.org | Scanned books, magazines, films | Rights differ per item |

## Using one in a project

1. Download the largest size and drop it on the project window.
2. Record where it came from: ask the Assistant "record the credit for stills/<file>: source <page address>,
   licence <as stated>, author <maker>".
3. Prefer public domain, CC0 or "no known restrictions". For anything else, read what the licence asks.

Your own material (screenshots of your sites, your logos, your photos) needs none of this.
