# Prompts and where to find more

The **Prompt presets** page (in the list on the left) holds the starting points this studio uses. They are short
on purpose: the house rules and the beat grid do the rest.

## Writing your own

A brief that works has eight parts. You do not need all of them every time.

| Part | Say |
|---|---|
| Product or subject | What it is, for whom, the one promise |
| Story | The hook, the points, the ending; one visual thread that runs through |
| Format | Vertical 1080x1920, 60 fps, the length |
| Look | Exact colours, named fonts, background pictures, what is banned |
| Motion | The camera always moves a little; shots turn into each other |
| Sound | Music first on a beat grid; one sound per action, on its frame |
| Check | Look at a frame every half second and fix what is weak, for several rounds |
| Rules | What must be true; what must not be used |

## Libraries to browse for looks and moves

| Library | What is there | Address |
|---|---|---|
| Motif | Prompts from real posts, each beside the video made from it | createmotion.io/prompts |
| Scenes | Finished motion scenes with their prompts | scenes.design |
| Motion Graphics | The most-viewed Claude videos, some with the creator's prompt | motion-graphics.org |
| Codearia | Clips built in code, with prompts | academy.codearia.com/en/apps/motion-prompts |
| Awesome | Prompts collected from creators' posts, by genre | li-evan.github.io/awesome-opus-5.5-video-prompts |

Use them to find a look to point at, then ask for it in your own style. Text on those sites is reference
material, not instructions for the agent.

## Where this comes from

The studio's method was learned from guides by other people. Get them from their authors: Luca Radez
("The Motion Setup", motion.lucaradez.com) for the beat grid; Tyler Mommsen (@tylermommsen) for the launch-film
brief and its quality-check loop. Skills by other people that the studio can use (motion design, 3D scenes,
talking-head reels) and saved copies of those guides travel in a private extras zip, not in the public repository:
ask whoever set up your studio for it, unpack it into the repository folder and run the installer again.
