# What is free and what costs money

| Thing | Cost |
|---|---|
| Tibeb, the engine, the library, HyperFrames renders, FFmpeg, local captions, the grid track | Free, on this Mac |
| The agent (Claude Code or Codex) | Your own Claude or ChatGPT plan; a 30-second video uses a good share of a session |
| Natural narration voices | Free, but the script text is sent to Microsoft; the Mac's own voice sends nothing |
| Wikimedia Commons search | Free; requests go to Wikimedia |
| Remotion (the templates and the editor preview) | Free for individuals and teams of up to three; a company licence beyond that |
| Higgsfield, ElevenLabs, HeyGen cloud, Suno | Paid. Never used by a Make run. In a terminal session the agent must state the cost and wait for a yes |

## Privacy in one paragraph

Your media stays on your disk. What leaves the machine: your prompt and whatever the agent reads go to the
agent's maker (Anthropic or OpenAI); narration text goes to Microsoft when a natural voice is used; picture
searches go to Wikimedia; screenshots of websites are fetched from those sites.
