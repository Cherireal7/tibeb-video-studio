---
title: Brand story
summary: A brand identity told in 30 seconds: marks, colours, type, the brand out in the world.
needs: logo variants, a brand board or guidelines, mock-up photos
---
A 30-second story of the [brand name] identity, on the beat grid, in the brand's own typeface and colours.
Material: the logo variants, brand board and mock-ups in this project's stills folder. Read the board for the facts (colour names and hex codes, typefaces, the idea behind the mark) and put nothing on screen that is not written there.
Order: the name, the marks (variants swapping on the hits), the idea in three shapes or words, the colours appearing one per hit, the typefaces set in the real fonts, the mock-ups piling up, one beat of silence, then every mark in a drop, then the credit.
Do not claim which logo came first or which was chosen unless the brief says so.
